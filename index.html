Dots Studio · Xiaohongshu Inc.

Fanxu Meng 孟繁续

I study foundation model architectures and how to make large models more capable and efficient.

I am currently with Dots Studio at Xiaohongshu Inc., where I conduct research on foundation model architectures. I received my Ph.D. from the Institute for Artificial Intelligence, Peking University, advised by Prof. Muhan Zhang. My research also includes parameter-efficient fine-tuning and efficient inference for long-context language models. I have served as a reviewer for NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, TPAMI, etc.

Before joining Peking University, I received my master’s degree from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Guangming Lu. I also spent over two years at Tencent YouTu as an intern and later as a full-time researcher, collaborating with Xing Sun, Hao Cheng, and Ke Li.
